# Getting help

- **Something does not work:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/nizwar/avo/issues/new/choose), or an **App compatibility** report for an app or game. Attach the diagnostics archive from **Help › Report a Problem** in Avo: it has the logs we need, with personal data removed.
- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/nizwar/avo/discussions).
- **Security problems:** report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

Avo is maintained by one person in their own time. Clear reports with steps
and diagnostics get answered first.
